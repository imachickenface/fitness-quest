Privacy

Fitness Quest · last updated 27 October 2026

Without an account

Everything you log (food, workouts, runs, weigh-ins, your profile and quests) is stored only in your browser on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere, except one lookup: when you scan a barcode, that number is sent to Open Food Facts to find the product.

With an account (optional)

If you sign in with Google, the same records are copied to a database at Supabase (servers in the United States) so you can use them on another phone. From your Google account the app receives only your email address and a Google account id, which are used to sign you in. Only you can read your records: the database refuses every other account.

What we don't do

No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and your data is never sold or shared. It is used only to show it back to you.

Deleting your data

In the app: Profile and targets > Your data. Delete account removes your account and every record stored with it. Data kept only on your phone is removed by clearing the site's data in your browser or uninstalling the app. You can save a copy of everything first with Save a backup.

Health information

Calorie and training targets come from standard formulas and are a starting point, not medical advice.
